## Visual grammar

### Geometry

- Build magic from concentric rings, offset ellipses, radial spokes, triangles and short rune-like marks.
- Rotate adjacent rings in opposite directions and at different speeds.
- Keep the center calm and legible; complexity increases toward the perimeter.
- Use controlled asymmetry: missing arcs, phase offsets and occasional broken fragments prevent the result from feeling like a generic HUD.

### Materials

- Base: near-black space (`#050607`) and oxidized bronze (`#6F5128`).
- Active magic: amber (`#F5A623`), hot gold (`#FFD36A`) and a near-white core (`#FFF4D0`).
- Time/Agamotto state only: emerald (`#39FF88`) with a pale mint core (`#D8FFE8`).
- Combine rough, engraved metal with additive light. Avoid making every surface transparent neon.

### Motion

1. **Idle:** rings drift slowly; sparse dust follows curved orbital paths.
2. **Thinking:** inner glyphs rotate faster, lines converge toward the center, amber shifts toward green.
3. **Speaking:** energy exits in radial pulses; short sparks peel off tangentially.
4. **Alert:** outer ring breaks into offset segments, then snaps back into alignment.

Use eased acceleration and staggered timing. A convincing spell feels assembled in layers, not switched on all at once.

## Translation to J-Core

- Preserve the existing cube silhouette so J-Core remains its own system.
- Place an original six-realm sigil on each cube face rather than copying Marvel glyphs.
- Add two thin orbital rings around the cube and a small internal energy core.
- Route neural/data lines through the cube edges; turn them amber while casting and emerald only for time-related actions.
- Let the cube open by separating face panels 3–6% instead of reproducing the Eye of Agamotto mechanism.
- Use one bright focal point and restrained bloom; most lines should stay dim until activated.

## Originality guardrails

- Do not use the Eye of Agamotto silhouette, Marvel rune arrangements, Doctor Strange logo, costume motifs or exact portal pattern.
- Derive new glyphs from J-Core concepts: six realms, neural routing, mission state, memory and temporal markers.
- Treat the references as principles of hierarchy, motion, material contrast and magical atmosphere.
